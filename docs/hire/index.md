## How it runs

1. **Your request to [info@wirt.ee](mailto:info@wirt.ee)**: what is broken or what you want built. One paragraph.
2. **The agreement.** Both parties agree what is delivered, when, for what price, and how unknowns are handled.
3. **The work.** During the progress you receive short written reports of what was done.
4. **Acceptance.** A recorded handover — demonstrated and documented.

I am busy. That is true and not entirely true: a large enough check makes me less busy in a hurry. Waiting costs nothing, except the start date.
