# Workshop

Everything here was designed and built from scratch, in my spare time, on a household budget: [solar](DIY/solar/index.md), [boiler](DIY/boiler/index.md), [mower](DIY/mower/index.md), [aquaponics](DIY/aquaponic/index.md), and the rest.

Nothing here is for sale. But read it as capability, not hobby: sensors, control loops, automation, integration. Systems designed, built and kept running by the same person who builds the professional ones. The boiler burns wood under a PID loop. That is no accident. My doctorate is in automatic control: the engineering of keeping a process at exactly the right settings. I do not sell fish tanks. But the automation that keeps one running, I can also build for your systems.
