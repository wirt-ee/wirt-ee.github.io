# On-prem AI

Open-weight models run on your hardware. The hardware decides the model size, not a contract. No API bills, no data leaving the rack.

**What I do:**

- **Size it.** Which models fit your GPUs and RAM — measured before you buy.
- **Serve it.** A serving stack on your metal, tuned to your load and power budget.
- **Keep it.** Monitoring and an upgrade path. A model you depend on must be upgradable.

**What I don't do:**

- Sell GPUs.
- Promise a model size your hardware cannot serve.

The production shape of the serving stack is public: [vLLM serving](../../logbook/vllm-serving/index.md).
